The Meaning Behind the Name

Two Flavours, One Kitchen

In Telugu, the finest meals live in the balance of sweetness and heat. Our kitchen is built on that idea — comfort and fire, side by side on every plate.

Bellam · ౉ెల్లం

Sweet

Jaggery, ghee and warmth — the traditional sweetness that rounds off a hearty Telugu meal and feels just like home.

Kaaram · కారం

Spicy

Bold red chilli, roasted spices and that unmistakable Andhra-Telangana heat that makes every bite come alive.
